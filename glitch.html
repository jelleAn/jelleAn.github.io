Glitch

Phainon Before the Cycle

The technique that I used for this glitch art was an app called Audacity in which I import the data and used the distoration effect to create the glitch best suited for this piece.

Phainon Cycle

In this piece I used Notepad++ to create a glitch, testing each variations until I get the glitch I find best suited.

The concept for my series of glitch arts is to represent how each cycles the character Phainon went through and in the end to slowly depict the transgression of being faceless.

Phainon Faceless

Same as Phainon before the Cycle I used Audacity in creating a glitch effect by selecting the reverb effect and kept on toggling how much

reverb will it impact the photo to the point the character will become completely faceless.
